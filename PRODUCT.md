# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Recruiters** skimming in under a minute: role fit, years of experience, stack, location and how to reach Shain.
- **Hiring engineers** judging depth: how the systems are designed, which trade-offs were made, and what Shain owned end to end.
- **Freelance clients** (founders, small teams) deciding whether to hire Shain for a build like MossMedAI or the Metadata Mapper.

All three arrive from a shared link (a job application, LinkedIn, a resume) and decide whether to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

A one-page portfolio for Shain Sethi that gets him interviews and freelance work. Success is a reader emailing him or downloading the resume after understanding what he builds and how he thinks.

## Positioning

A full stack engineer who designs the whole request path, from the React screen to the AWS infrastructure under it, and has shipped it in production. Full stack leads; frontend, DevOps and AI engineering are named strengths, not separate identities. The proof is real systems with real numbers and real trade-offs, not a list of technologies.

## Operating Context

- Static HTML, CSS and JS, no framework or build step, served by GitHub Pages from `main` at https://shainsethi.github.io/ through `.github/workflows/pages.yml` (`deploy-pages@v5`).
- Read on phones (links from LinkedIn and job sites) and on desktop (hiring engineers reviewing an application).
- The main actions are "Email me" (mailto, shainsethi1000@gmail.com) and downloading `assets/Shain_Sethi_Resume.pdf`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Work location:** remote first, open to relocating abroad for the right role. No city is presented as where he wants to work. Bengaluru appears only as a fact of past and current jobs.
- **Privacy:** no screenshots of client or employer apps (MossMedAI, Metadata Mapper, Taabi fleet dashboards). Their interfaces may be shown only as drawn HTML mock-ups with generic, non-client data, and their architecture only at the level already public on the site.
- **No live demos:** the Oil Drilling Dashboard and FlowForge demos are broken (empty charts, "Connection error", blank canvas) and must not be linked until fixed. Their code repositories may be linked.
- **Terminology:** "MossMedAI" (hospital platform, 14 NestJS services), "Metadata Mapper" (LLM schema-mapping pipeline), "Taabi Mobility, a CEAT company".

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Shain Sethi. Voice: first person, plain and technical, no hype.
- Roles named on the site: full stack (leading), frontend, DevOps, AI engineer.
- Real tool logos keep their own colours and carry the trademark line in the footer.

## Product Principles

1. Show systems, not skills lists: every claim sits next to the system or number that proves it.
2. A recruiter gets role, years, stack and contact within the first screen; depth is there for engineers who keep reading.
3. Privacy before polish: never trade a client's or employer's confidentiality for a better picture.
4. Every link works. A broken demo is worse than no demo.
5. Full stack leads; the other three roles support it, never compete with it.
